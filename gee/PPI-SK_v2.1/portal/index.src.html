Details for the register

How the check works

The treatment polygons are overlaid on the 829 FRPIM blocks. For every block they touch, the page measures the overlapping hectares and adds them up by priority phase, need class, feasibility class and FRPIM's recommended intervention.

Colour of the check

  • Green at least 60 % of the treated area is in Phase 1-2 and at most 5 % is Protected or Excluded land.
  • Red more than 20 % is Protected or Excluded land, or more than half is in Phase 4-5, or most of the area lies outside the range blocks.
  • Amber everything in between. Review it in the field.

Who decides

FRPIM is decision support. The proposing officer uploads the map; the ACF records a recommendation; the DCF approves, approves with a recorded deviation, or returns it. Approving an Amber or Red proposal needs a written reason, so every departure from the model is documented.

Access

  • Range staff (Contributors) can check maps, submit their own proposals and delete them before a decision.
  • ACF and DCF (Editors, and the owner) can record decisions and remove any entry.
  • Viewers can read the register.

Model data: FRPIM v2.2 block table of 19 Nov 2026. Classes are quintiles within the range. Areas are geodesic hectares. Treatment geometry is stored simplified (to about 2 m) in the register.
